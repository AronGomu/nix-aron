Why this shape

  • T1 first — all human-in-loop work frontloaded.
  • T2 sole declarer of users.
  • T3 ∥ T5 — no edge between → parallel-safe.
  • T6 join — consumes both contracts, freeze before spawn.
  • Acyclic, topologically valid.
